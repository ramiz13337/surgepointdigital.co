The plan / 05

Close the gap. Five moves.

01

Rebuild the site on a real structure

Keep the brand exactly as it is, put an engine under it. A page for every service you sell, a page for every town you serve: Rocklin, Roseville, Granite Bay, Loomis, Lincoln, Folsom, El Dorado Hills. That is the same inventory GVD used to get to 16,381 keywords, built at your quality level instead of theirs.

02

Move the reviews to where they count

Thirteen clients already wrote the copy. One request to each of them, plus a standing ask at project close, and those raves land on Google and Houzz where the map pack can see them. This is the fastest lever on the list and it costs you nothing but the asks.

03

Own the ADU question

Nobody in this market has written the honest guide to the new 60 day approval rule, the county preapproved plans, or what an ADU actually costs in Placer County. Every one of those searches is a homeowner with a permit and a budget. It compounds every month it runs.

04

Turn projects into proof

You have three named projects and photography worth showing. Each one becomes a case study with the problem, the spec, the trades, and the finish. That is what a $120,000 kitchen client reads before calling, and it is the content that makes the city pages rank.

05

Paid where the intent is highest

Google Local Services and a tight search campaign on the high intent terms, so you show up while the organic work compounds. At a $41,500 average ticket the math on a qualified lead is not close.
