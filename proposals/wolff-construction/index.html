The work / 05

What I'd actually do.

01

Build the pages that don't exist yet

A page for every job you do and every town you do it in. Kitchens in Granite Bay. ADUs in Loomis. Whole home in Roseville. It's a one time build, and it's the difference between eight pages and a few hundred.

02

Claim and fill out the Google profile

Then get the reviews onto it. Thirteen clients already wrote the words. One message to each of them, then a standing ask at the end of every job from here on.

03

Answer what people are already typing

What a kitchen really costs here. How long an ADU takes. What goes wrong and how you handle it. Those are the actual searches, and nobody local has answered them honestly.

04

Fix what's under the hood

Page titles, load speed, how Google reads your project pages, and making sure your name, address and phone match everywhere they show up online. Unglamorous. It's also why some sites rank and identical ones don't.

05

Show you what's working

One page you can open whenever you want: what you rank for, what moved this month, and which calls came from search.
