The plan

Launch right. Five moves.

01

Website build (the foundation)

Not a one page site. A page for every service you do: renewals, title transfers, out of state, lost stickers, VIN checks, insurance. Plus the DMV forms library that took Tags Clinic from invisible to 5,600 keywords. Same blueprint, your side of town.

02

Review engine (Yelp + Google)

People already love this shop, the reviews prove it. One text to every happy customer and you're stacking 10 to 15 new reviews a month. That's the single biggest lever for showing up on the map. Also fixes the name confusion up the street.

03

SEO (monthly, ongoing)

Keep publishing what people search: expired tags, late fees, smog questions, out of state moves. This is how Tags Clinic went from 0 to 5,634 keywords in 18 months. It compounds every month it runs.

04

Email / SMS campaigns (remarketing)

Registration renews every single year. One "your tags are due soon, we got you" text and last year's customer is this year's customer. Nobody in this market is doing it.

05

Paid ads (Google + others)

NeedTags is proving these clicks are worth $300K a month, but they can't serve a walk in. We run ads on the high intent local searches and let them pay to educate the market for you.
