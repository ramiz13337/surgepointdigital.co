Fleets / 06

The page for businesses with trucks.

Pest control, plumbing, HVAC, delivery, property management, dealer lots. A company with 15 trucks has 15 renewals a year and nobody whose job it is to handle them. That's a customer who signs up once and renews forever.

After launch / 07

What the monthly SEO program does.

Tags Clinic went from ranking for nothing to 5,634 keywords in 18 months doing exactly this, every month, without stopping. Three parts.

Part 1 · Growing your market share
new pages every month, each aimed at a search with a customer behind it
01
The forms library

A page for every DMV form people Google in a panic: REG 5, REG 101, REG 156 and dozens more. This exact play built Tags Clinic's entire lead. Every one published is another door into your shop.

02
Panic pages

Expired tags, late fees, registration holds, failed smog. People search these at 11pm, stressed, ready to pay whoever answers first. That gets to be you.

03
En Español

Full Spanish versions of the money pages. Huge demand in this county and almost nobody serves it properly. Near zero competition for entire neighborhoods of customers.

04
Neighborhood pages

Mira Mesa, Sorrento Valley, Scripps Ranch, Kearny Mesa, UTC. Every "dmv services near me" within ten minutes of the shop becomes your search result.

Part 2 · Getting Google to trust you
the signals, outside your site, that decide who shows up on the map
05
Listings that all match

Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing, BBB and the local directories, all claimed, all saying the same name, address and phone. Mismatched listings quietly kill map rankings. Yours get made airtight.

06
Local links

Dealer partners, local business groups and local press pointing at your site. Every link is a vote that you're the real one on Miramar Rd.

07
An active Google profile

Posts, photos and answered questions every month. Google shows busy profiles above dormant ones, and most shops let theirs go quiet.

PROGRAM 02 Email / SMS

Registration renews every single year, so last year's customer is this year's customer. One "your tags are due soon" text at the right time. Nobody in this market does it.

PROGRAM 03 Review engine

Every happy customer gets one text asking for a review, and every review gets answered. 10 to 15 new five star reviews a month. The single biggest lever for the map.

Most shops never get this.

A monthly read on the business: where calls come from, which pages make the phone ring, what each competitor gained or lost, and what happens next because of it. The same competitor research that produced this plan, running for you every month.
