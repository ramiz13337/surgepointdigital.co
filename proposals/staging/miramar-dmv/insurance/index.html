Common questions

Questions we get every day.

Do I need insurance before I can register?
Yes. California requires proof of financial responsibility, and the registration can't be completed without it. That's exactly why doing both here saves a trip.
Can you insure me the same day?
In most cases yes. Call or come in and we can usually get coverage in place while you're here.
What if I've had a lapse in coverage?
A lapse can trigger a registration suspension, which then has to be cleared. Come in and we'll tell you where you stand and what it takes to fix.
Do you handle commercial vehicles?
Yes. If you run vehicles for a business, talk to us about covering the fleet and handling the registrations together.
