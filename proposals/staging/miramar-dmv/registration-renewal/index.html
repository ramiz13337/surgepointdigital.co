Common questions

Questions we get every day.

My tags expired months ago. Can you still help?
Yes. Late registration is one of the most common things we handle. The DMV adds penalties the longer it sits, so the sooner you come in the less it costs, but there is no point where we stop being able to help.
Do I need a smog check first?
Most vehicles need one every other year, and newer cars are often exempt. Give us the plate number and we'll tell you whether one is due before you spend money on it.
I never got a renewal notice.
Not a problem. We can pull up the registration with your plate number and your ID. Notices go missing constantly, usually because the address on file is old, and we can fix that at the same time.
There's a hold on my registration.
Unpaid parking tickets, toll violations and insurance lapses all cause holds. We can see what the hold is when we look up the plate and tell you exactly what has to clear before the renewal can go through.
Can I do the whole thing online?
You can start it online and pay online. The sticker still has to be handed to you or mailed, so there's one pickup, or we'll post it to the address on file.

Renewing your vehicle registration in San Diego

A California registration renewal is due every year, and the state posts a notice roughly two months before the expiration date printed on your sticker. If that notice never arrived, it almost always means the address on your record is out of date, which we can correct at the same time as the renewal.

You do not need the notice to renew. The plate number and a photo ID are enough for us to pull the record. What we look at first is whether anything is blocking the transaction: unpaid parking citations, toll violations, an insurance lapse reported to the DMV, or a smog check that has come due. Any of those will stop a renewal, and we would rather tell you at the counter than take payment for something that cannot complete.

Smog catches people out most often. Most vehicles in San Diego County need a check every other year, newer cars are generally exempt for their first several years, and the requirement is tied to the vehicle rather than to you. Give us the plate and we will tell you whether one is due before you pay for a test you did not need.

Expired registration is not a problem we turn away, and it is a large part of what we do. California adds penalties that grow the longer a registration sits lapsed, so the cost rises with time, but there is no point at which we stop being able to help. Whether your tags expired last week or two years ago, bring the plate number in and we will work out exactly what it takes to get current.

If you would rather not come in at all, start the renewal online. It takes about two minutes, and we will text you the moment the sticker is ready rather than leaving you to wonder whether it went through.

One question worth answering before you drive over: do you need the sticker in your hand today, or is the post fine? If you are being pulled over for expired tags, or you have a trip coming up, collect it here and put it on the plate before you leave. If there is no urgency, we can post it to the address on the record. Either way the registration itself is active from the moment we process it, not from the moment the sticker arrives.
