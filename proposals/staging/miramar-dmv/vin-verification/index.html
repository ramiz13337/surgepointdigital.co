Common questions

Questions we get every day.

Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk in during opening hours. It's quick enough that we can usually take you straight away.
When do I need a VIN verification?
Most commonly when bringing a vehicle in from another state. Also for some title transfers, vehicles that have been off the road a long time, and rebuilt or specially constructed vehicles.
How long does it take?
The verification itself is a few minutes. If you're doing a full out of state conversion at the same time, budget a bit longer for the paperwork.
Can you come to me?
We handle verifications here in the office. For businesses running several vehicles, call us and we'll work something out for the fleet.
What if the VIN plate is damaged or missing?
That becomes a different process involving CHP rather than a standard verification. Come in and we'll point you the right way.
Good to know

When you need a VIN verification

A VIN verification confirms the vehicle in front of the examiner matches the documents presented for it. It is recorded on form REG 31.

Most people need one because they have brought a vehicle in from another state. It also comes up for vehicles that have been off the road a long time, for some title transfers, and for rebuilt or specially constructed vehicles.
