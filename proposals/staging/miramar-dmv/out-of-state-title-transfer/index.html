Before you come in

What you'll need to bring.

Everything for a conversion has to go in at once, so it is worth checking this list before you drive over.

Financed vehicles take longer because the lender holds the title. Come in and we'll start the request for you.

  • The out of state title, or the lender's information if it's financed
  • The vehicle itself, for the VIN verification
  • A smog certificate from a California station
  • Your California ID or driver's licence, or proof of residency
  • Proof of insurance that meets California minimums
  • Current out of state registration if you still have it
Common questions

Questions we get every day.

When does the 20 days actually start?
When you become a California resident, which the DMV ties to things like starting a job here, renting or buying a home, or enrolling children in school. It is not the day you drove across the border.
My car is financed and the bank has the title.
Very common. The lender has to send the title to California DMV, and we start that process for you. Come in and we'll get the request moving so the clock isn't running while you wait.
Do I need a smog check?
Almost always, yes, and it has to be from a California licensed station. There are narrow exemptions for very new vehicles and some others. Ask us before you pay for one.
What is a VIN verification and why do I need one?
California verifies that the vehicle in front of them matches the title before issuing plates. We do it here in the office while you wait, so it is not a separate errand.
Can I keep my out of state plates?
No. Once you register in California you get California plates and surrender the old ones or dispose of them as your previous state requires.
