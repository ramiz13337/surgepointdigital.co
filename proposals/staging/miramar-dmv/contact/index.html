Finding us on Miramar Rd

We are at 7094 Miramar Rd, suite 102, San Diego, CA 92121, with free parking directly in front of the office. No appointment is needed and there is no queue ticket system. Most visits take about ten minutes from walking in to walking out.

We are open Monday to Thursday from 9:30am to 5:30pm, and on Friday from 9:30am to 1:00pm and again from 2:00pm to 5:00pm. If you are arriving close to closing, call ahead and we will tell you honestly whether to hurry or come back tomorrow.

The office is convenient to Mira Mesa, Sorrento Valley, Scripps Ranch, Kearny Mesa and UTC, and Miramar Rd is straightforward to reach from both the 805 and the 15. Most people find us quicker to get to than the nearest field office, before accounting for the difference in what happens once you arrive.

The single most useful thing you can do before visiting is call with your plate number. In about a minute we can pull the record, see whether a smog check is due, check for holds, and tell you exactly what to bring and what it will cost. It saves far more time than it takes.

If you only need a registration renewal, you do not need to come in at all. Start it online, and we will text you when the sticker is ready to collect.

If you are coming for anything other than a straightforward renewal, bring more than you think you need. Extra paperwork costs you nothing to carry and a missing document costs you the trip. Title, registration, photo ID, proof of insurance and any letter the DMV has sent you covers almost every situation we see walk through the door.

Parking is the small detail that makes a bigger difference than it should. The spaces in front of the office are free and unmetered, so you are not feeding a meter or watching the clock while you are inside. For anyone bringing a vehicle in for a VIN verification, there is room to pull in and be seen without blocking the lot.
