Good to know

What a licensed DMV partner actually means

The state authorises us to process registration on our own system. Your transaction is completed and issued at our counter, not collected and forwarded somewhere else.

That is why people searching for DMV services in San Diego end up here instead of booking a field office appointment three weeks out. We handle registration renewals, title transfers, out of state conversions, VIN verification and replacement stickers. Because we are also an insurance office, coverage and registration can happen in the same sitting.

Read more

Renewals are the one thing you can start online. Everything else needs the paperwork or the vehicle in front of us, which is why those pages ask you to come in. We are a short drive from Mira Mesa, Sorrento Valley, Scripps Ranch, Kearny Mesa and UTC, with free parking out front.

Most people find us after looking at a field office appointment three weeks out, or after being told to come back with a form nobody mentioned the first time. The fees are identical either way, because the state sets those. What changes is that you are not taking a morning off work for it.
