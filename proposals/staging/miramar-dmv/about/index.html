How this office runs

Being a licensed DMV business partner is a formal authorisation from the state. It is what lets us complete the transaction here and get your sticker moving the same day, rather than queueing behind the DMV's own mail.

The difference people actually notice is smaller than that. Someone answers the phone. If you describe your situation before driving over, you get a straight answer about what to bring. If something is going to be a problem, you hear it at the counter rather than after paying.

Our reviews mention John and Eddie by name more often than they mention the business, which tells you most of what you need to know. One thing worth repeating: a separate business with a similar name sits further along Miramar Rd. We are at 7094, suite 102.

We are not trying to be the cheapest option on this road, and we would say so plainly. What we offer is that the transaction is finished correctly the first time, by someone who does this all day and will tell you before you pay if something is not going to work.

For most people the value is not the fee. It is not having to come back a second time with the one document nobody mentioned.
